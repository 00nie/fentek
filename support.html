Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI satellite estimating?

+

Our satellite estimating is typically within 2-3% of hand measurements on flat terrain. The system uses high-resolution aerial imagery combined with property boundary data and elevation models. For complex terrain or heavily wooded lots, we recommend a site visit to verify, but for 70-80% of residential jobs, satellite estimating is accurate enough to send a competitive quote.

How long is the free trial?

+

The free trial lasts 14 days and includes full access to all features on the Pro plan. No credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial, you can choose a plan that fits your business or your account will simply be paused — we never charge you without your consent.

Can I cancel anytime?

+

Yes. FenTek is month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll retain access through the end of your current billing period, and you can export all your data before your account is closed.

Does FenTek work with QuickBooks?

+

Yes. FenTek integrates with QuickBooks Online for two-way syncing of invoices, payments, and expenses. Once connected, your financial data flows automatically between the two platforms — no more double data entry. The QuickBooks integration is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans.

What fence types does the estimating engine support?

+

FenTek supports all major fence types including wood privacy (board-on-board, dog-ear, shadow box), chain link, vinyl/PVC, aluminum ornamental, wrought iron, composite, and horizontal slat. Each type has its own material breakdown engine that calculates posts, rails, panels, concrete, hardware, and gates based on your custom pricing tables.

How many users can I have on my account?

+

The Starter plan supports up to 2 crew members. The Pro plan supports unlimited crew members. The Enterprise plan includes unlimited users plus multi-location support. All crew members get access to the mobile app for viewing their daily jobs, routes, and materials lists.

Is my data secure?

+

Yes. FenTek uses industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL in transit, AES-256 at rest), secure cloud infrastructure, and regular security audits. Your customer data and business information are never shared with other users. You can read our full privacy policy for details on how we handle and protect your data.

Can I import my existing customer data?

+

Yes. You can import customers, contacts, and job history via CSV upload during onboarding. If you're coming from another platform like Jobber or a spreadsheet, our support team can help you migrate your data for free during your first 30 days.
