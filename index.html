How it works

From address to accurate quote in 5 minutes

No site visit. No spreadsheet. No guesswork.

1

Enter the address

Customer gives you an address. FenTek pulls up satellite imagery, property boundaries, and terrain data automatically.

2

Draw the fence line

Trace the fence path on the interactive map. We calculate linear footage, post count, and gate placements as you draw. Terrain and slope adjustments happen automatically.

3

Pick your materials

Choose the fence type from your pricing tables. The system generates a complete materials list and labor estimate using your rates and markups.

4

Send the quote

A branded, professional quote goes out instantly — as a PDF or interactive link. The customer can view, approve, and e-sign from their phone. You just won the job before your competitor even drove out to measure.
